# 1. Long-Term Goal

Build and demonstrate a **production-grade AI marketing system** that:

* Discovers trends → generates content → distributes → learns → improves
* Evolves from **simple automation → agentic workflows → full orchestration**
* Teaches marketers how to move from execution → system design

Context: Most companies are still stuck at task automation, while only a minority reach orchestration maturity ([Zapier][1])

---

# 2. Persona / Audience

**Primary Persona: Brian Wood**

* Director of Marketing Operations & Technology
* 20+ years experience
* Manages systems, vendors, and execution teams
* Likely stack: CRM + automation + analytics + content ops

### What Brian cares about:

* Efficiency (time saved)
* Scalability (repeatable systems)
* Control (governance, approvals)
* ROI (measurable impact)

### What he does NOT care about:

* AI hype
* Toy demos
* Over-engineered systems without business value

# 3. Series Title + Summary

### Title

**“From Tasks to Systems: Building an AI Marketing Engine”**

### Summary

A step-by-step series showing how to evolve from manual marketing work into a fully automated, AI-driven system that discovers trends, generates content, distributes across channels, and continuously improves through feedback.
